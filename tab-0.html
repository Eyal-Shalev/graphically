the motivation

“To give a thing a name, a label, a handle; to rescue it from anonymity, to pluck it out of the Place of Namelessness, in short to identify it - well, that’s a way of bringing the said thing into being.” 

haroun and the sea of stories, by salman rushdie
 

Being a designer – as is the case for any other artist – compels a search for unlimited sources of ideas and inspiration. 

 

Being a designer is something intrinsic, like a second skin. It is a way of observing, learning, knowing, listening, and asking the right questions.

 

GR@PHICALLY has expertise and extensive experience in branding and identity design; implementing marketing strategies for organizations and businesses of all kinds; identifying fundamental issues, and achieving ambitious goals.

 

Among the diverse clients of GR@PHICALLY are successful enterprises in cosmetics, medicine, logistics, education, security, technology, law, architecture, and many other fields.

 

Founded in 2005 by Yona Kidron, graduate in art and design of Bezalel Academy, Jerusalem, Israel. GR@PHICALLY is now based in Los Angeles, and is actively securing new projects and clients.
